# Aprender con ciencia Series Specification

This is ERC Academy's numbered science-informed English-learning content series.

Each normal post contains the ERC Academy header, series number, strong headline, one educational claim, short explanation, a practical PROBALO activity, learner-facing CTA, source credit when applicable, ercacademynic.com, and a corresponding Spanish Instagram caption.

Explain one idea per post. Translate learning science into practical English-learning behavior. Avoid overclaiming and distinguish research findings from ERC Academy adaptations. Keep poster copy simpler than caption copy.

Rotate topics among listening, retrieval, memory, pronunciation, vocabulary, confidence and beliefs, CEFR abilities, study planning, speaking tasks, attention, deliberate practice, feedback, repetition, multimodal learning and habit design.

Always read POST_LOG.md before generating. Do not infer publication status from numbering alone.
