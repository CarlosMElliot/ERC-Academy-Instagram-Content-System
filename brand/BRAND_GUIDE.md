# ERC Academy Brand Guide

## Purpose
Canonical visual/editorial reference for ERC Academy social content, especially **Aprender con ciencia**.

## Core visual identity
- Deep navy / royal-blue gradient background.
- Electric-blue/cyan diagonal framing and geometric accents.
- Restrained gold/yellow accents and thin gold dividers.
- `ERC ACADEMY` header in white uppercase with generous tracking.
- Series line: `APRENDER CON CIENCIA • [NUMBER]` (historical artwork may use APRENDE; preserve an approved design when continuing it).
- Oversized heavy sans-serif headline, usually mixing cyan and white.
- Concise, mobile-readable Spanish body copy.
- Cyan `PROBALO` practical-action section.
- Strong cyan CTA near the bottom, often with a learner-facing question and `Contanos en los comentarios`.
- Source/research credit at bottom-left when applicable.
- `ercacademynic.com` at bottom-right.

## Tone
Professional, scientific, energetic, modern and educational. Never childish.

## Lighting and grading
- Dark studio mood.
- Restrained brightness/exposure and contrast.
- Do not increase saturation, HDR, glow or exposure beyond approved samples.
- Subtle blue rim lighting is acceptable.

## Composition
- Rotate subject placement left/right where practical.
- Leave clean negative space for headlines.
- Props/icons must explain the learning concept rather than decorate randomly.
- Keep text readable on a phone.
- Never crowd CTA/footer.
- For standard Instagram feed artwork, default to 4:5 portrait.

## Spanish voice
Natural Spanish with Nicaraguan voseo when appropriate. Preferred forms include:
- escuchá
- probalo
- contanos
- recordá
- elegí
- explicá
- planificá
- comprobá

## Caption philosophy
Every Instagram post gets a Spanish caption. The caption should expand the idea rather than repeat every line on the poster. A common structure is: hook, concise explanation, practical application, question/CTA, source/research context when applicable, then relevant hashtags.

Common hashtags used historically include `#ERCAcademy`, `#AprenderIngles`, `#AprenderConCiencia`, and `#EnglishLearning`.
